## Findings

1. **Material — B2's blocked-hint scenario names two viewport sizes that both sit *above* the floor they are meant to be under, so neither half of the harness check can reach the state it tests.** Spec:336-339 stages "at 700px wide ⌃⌘B on a collapsed navigation leaves it collapsed" and "the same at 500px tall for the context shelf". The body group's floor is `navMin` 160 + one 1px separator + `centerMinWidth` 320 + 1px + `inspectorMin` 200 = **682px** (`packages/tokens/src/lib/sizes.ts:18-27`, separator `--cs-p-line: 1px` at `packages/tokens/src/tokens.css:39`), so a 700px window has 18px of slack and the nav does not merely expand — at a fresh 700px mount it never collapses in the first place, because `navDefault` 20% clamps up to its 160px minimum and the centre still clears 320. The repo's own evidence agrees: `cockpit.mjs:70-72` needs **600px** to squeeze the nav to a rail and `:275` uses **560px** for the existing "an expand with no room to act" case. The height half fails the same way: at 500px tall the body is 500 − 48 − 1 = 451px against a centre-group floor of `mainMinHeight` 240 + 1 + `contextMin` 120 = **361px**, so the shelf mounts expanded at its 180px default with 90px to spare. A wave-2 author writes both assertions, watches both go green for the wrong reason (the toggle acted, no hint appeared, nothing asserted the hint's *absence*), and ships a blocked state no test ever entered.

2. **Material — the two-second `hintAt` window makes the same scenario's clearing assertion undecidable, and two seconds is the one number in the spec with no home.** Spec:88 has each new `hintAt` open the tooltip "for two seconds without hover"; spec:336-338 then asserts that after resizing to 1440 "the tooltip is gone and ⌃⌘B acts". The tooltip closes after two seconds *whether or not `blocked` cleared*, and the harness's own idioms put the read well inside that window — spec:340-341's duration+100ms after the toggle plus the 400ms this harness already sleeps after every `setViewportSize` (`cockpit.mjs:71,75`) is ~700ms — so a green "the tooltip is gone" proves nothing about the clear condition, and a red one is a race. Underneath sits an unstated behaviour: spec:264-265 says `blocked` clears on resize but never says whether clearing *retracts* an already-open tooltip, which is precisely what the assertion reads. And two seconds appears only in prose — it is absent from the CSS contract table (spec:165-181), so the harness must carry its own copy of the number, the exact duplication spec:341-342 forbids for the slide ("so the harness has no second copy of the number") and that B12 fixed for the peek by adding `--panel-peek-open`/`--panel-peek-close`. A `--panel-hint-duration` property and one clause on retraction close both.

3. **Material — B3's step vocabulary describes five of its six kinds and writes three of its nine views, and the kind it leaves undescribed is the one carrying both zero-tolerance mid-slide baselines.** Spec:366-372 promises "Six step kinds, and every named view is written in them", then gives `menu`, `select`, `toggle` and `dwell` a verb each and gives `['pause-slide', region, 0.5]` none. Nothing says whether `pause-slide` performs the toggle itself or pauses a slide a previous step started — which means `slide-nav-hide` and `slide-nav-show` are, as written, the identical step `['pause-slide','nav',0.5]`, distinguishable only by a preceding `['toggle','nav']` the spec never writes. The recipe at spec:376-381 describes the pausing in prose but is never bound to the step's two arguments (`region`, `0.5`). Only `view-open`, `when-hidden-open` and the dwell of `peek-all` are actually written out; `top-collapsed`, `hidden-all`, `rail-all` and both slide frames are not, and `rail-all`/`peek-all` additionally need variant-selection steps through the `When hidden` submenu before their toggles, which no example shows. The interpreter author invents the convention for the two views photographed at `maxDiffPixels: 0` (`tools/src/visual/playwright.config.mts:60`, with a comment refusing any tolerance).

4. **Material — B8's landmark fix landed in the props section and not in the testing contract, so the spec now contradicts itself about the rail's accessible name.** Spec:131-134 correctly renders the rail as `<section aria-label={label}>` holding a control named `Expand <label>`, and cites `cockpit.mjs:205` as the reason. But the unit contract at spec:324 still reads "`PanelEdge` renders nothing for `hidden`, **a landmark named `Expand <region>` for `rail`**, a zone for `peek`" — the exact wording B8 flagged. `cockpit.mjs:205` locates the rail as `getByRole('region', { name: 'Navigation' })` containing `getByRole('button', { name: 'Expand navigation' })`; a writer implementing the testing contract rather than the prose names the landmark "Expand navigation" and turns that assertion red in wave 2. The testing contract is what an implementer builds against, so this is the copy that matters.

5. **Material — the variant map still cannot deliver the seam it is quoted for: a `mini` variant needs a label the map does not hold, and the seam bullet contradicts itself in consecutive clauses.** Spec:141-143 says each map entry "names its renderer **and its shell behaviour**" — renderer and `collapse`, nothing else. But the menu shows variants as human strings ("The accepted values are exactly `hidden`, `rail`, `peek`; the menu shows them as Vanish, Rail, Peek", spec:43-44) with no stated home, and spec:415-417 then lists a new variant's cost as "one entry in `PanelEdge`'s map …, a part if it needs one, **one radio item**, one composite view" immediately before asserting "the shell, the preference validator (the map's keys) and **the menu code do not change**." A radio item is menu code. So `mini` at 24px is: a map entry, plus a label somewhere the map does not define, plus a menu edit — and the map is never said to be exported from the kit at all, though spec:283-285 has `usePanelVariant` validate against its keys and the preset read `collapse` from its entries. Adding a `label` to the map entry and naming its export makes the sentence true; leaving it as is recreates the two-lists-that-can-drift defect B11 was fixed for, with no matching one-line spec.

6. **Material — A1's "any pointerdown inside the group" disarm cancels the slide on an ordinary click into the manuscript, and the spec's own rationale says it costs nothing.** Spec:248-252 disarms on "any `pointerdown` inside the group element, heard at capture … a pointer down anywhere in the group during a 200ms slide costs nothing to honour." The body group's element contains `#nav`, `#center` and `#inspector`, and `main` — the manuscript — sits inside `#center`'s nested cockpit (`studio-cockpit.tsx:118-135`), therefore inside the body group element. So a writer who presses ⌃⌘B and clicks into their text 100ms later has `data-sliding` removed mid-transition; removing the `transition-property` cancels the running transition and the panel snaps to its end width instead of easing. That is not "nothing" — it is the visible jump the whole *Motion* section exists to prevent, and spec:354-356 asserts the behaviour as correct without anyone having decided that a click in the manuscript should abort the animation. The narrow fix is to disarm on a pointerdown whose target is inside the group but *not* inside a `[data-panel]` content region, or to accept it explicitly in the risk list rather than in a parenthetical that denies the cost.

8. **Minor — the blocked announcement is the only announcement in the spec with no wording, and two blocked regions at once are undefined in all three channels.** Spec:59-62 gives exact strings for every other case ("Navigation hidden" / "Navigation shown", "Top shelf collapsed" / "Top shelf expanded"); spec:259-261 says only that "the live region says the same", where "the same" is the hint — "No room: widen the window" — which contains no region name. 10. **Minor — the hint gets no picture anywhere, and `resize` is an enumerated step kind that no named view uses.** The bounded set is nine views (spec:372-375; 9 × 2 schemes = the promised 18), and none of them resizes: `cockpit`, `view-open`, `top-collapsed`, `hidden-all`, `rail-all`, `peek-all`, `when-hidden-open` and the two slide frames all run at the config viewport. 11. **Minor — the peek's dwell reintroduces the second copy of a number B12 had just given a property.** B12 added `--panel-peek-open` / `--panel-peek-close` (spec:180) precisely so a skin could retune the peek, and spec:371 then has `peek-all` dwell "for the open delay plus 100ms" as a literal `ms` argument in `baselines.mjs` — which the prune script imports under plain Node (spec:364-365) and so cannot read a CSS custom property from a page. ## Verdict
